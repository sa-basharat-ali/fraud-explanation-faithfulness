The part I got wrong

My first scoring counted every reason outside the model's top 3 as unfaithful: 40 of 40 explanations wrong, 129 of 240 reasons invented. Too harsh, since 107 were real, smaller contributions. Splitting "real but minor" from "held against fraud" gave the honest number, 16 of 40. A keyword scan also flagged 8 grounded explanations, all false alarms: "email address" matched my address-velocity pattern.
